# XPENG (Xiaopeng Motors)

> XPENG is a Chinese smart electric-vehicle company headquartered in Guangzhou, designing and manufacturing smart EVs (G6, G7, G9, P7/P7+, X9, MONA M03) with in-house advanced driver-assistance and in-car software. It is listed on the NYSE (XPEV) and HKEX (9868). XPENG publishes no public developer API: its open platform (open.xiaopeng.com) is geo-restricted and account-gated, and no public API documentation, OpenAPI specs, or client SDKs were found.
